We don't forecast sunshine.
We forecast what it costs the grid.

IntelliGen predicts solar and wind generation 24–72 hours out, quantifies how uncertain that forecast really is, and tells operators exactly when to charge storage, export, or prep backup — before a surplus or deficit ever hits the grid.

The Gap

A number isn't a decision.

Most forecasting tools stop at the prediction. Operators need the prediction priced against what the grid can actually absorb.

Conventional forecast

"Expected solar generation at 16:00 = 600 MW."

IntelliGen

Expected = 600 MW. Plausible range = 520–680 MW. Demand = 850 MW. Battery available = 120 MW. Backup available = 100 MW.

Deficit risk: HIGH. Prepare additional flexibility.

The Intelligence Loop

Forecast → understand risk → recommend action.

Three engines, each answering one question a control room actually asks.

01

Forecast

Persistence baseline and XGBoost estimate solar (and later wind) output for every future interval, 24–72 hours ahead.

02

Risk

Forecast uncertainty is weighed against demand, battery headroom, export limits and backup capacity to classify exposure.

03

Action

Charge storage, export, shift load, curtail as last resort — or discharge, prepare backup, escalate. Ranked, not guessed.

Why Not Just Another Forecast

The layer everyone skips.

Forecasting renewables isn't new. Connecting the forecast, its uncertainty and the grid's real flexibility into one recommendation is.

Display predicted output→Translate output into operational risk

⚠ Decision-support, not autonomous control. IntelliGen never touches generators, batteries or grid infrastructure directly — it forecasts, assesses risk, and recommends. The authorized operator makes the call.
